Panorama Gothic Seal Example

64 experimental Small Seal glyphs: U+3D000 through U+3D03F.
Drawn with image_gen.imagegen from the official Unicode Small Seal code-chart reference, then traced into smooth TrueType outlines. Original chart fonts were not extracted.
Only character strokes appear in the glyphs; labels and chart grids are not part of the font.
This is a design sample, not the completed Unicode 19 font family.
